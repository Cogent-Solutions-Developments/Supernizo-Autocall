# Product scope — V1

Supernizo Autocall is a real-time visitor intelligence platform for teams that operate one or more websites. It shows authorized agents who is currently active, supplies a privacy-conscious session timeline, and lets an agent chat with or invite a visitor to a browser-based voice or video call.

## Goals

- Give agents timely, useful context about a visitor's current session without inferring a person's identity from their IP address.
- Enable a visitor-controlled route from a website session to chat, voice, or video conversation.
- Support multiple customer sites while keeping each site's data, allowed origins, and agents isolated.

## V1 — in scope

### Site tracking and session intelligence

- Multi-site website tracking with a site-specific public key and allowed-origin configuration.
- Anonymous visitor and session identity generated by the tracker; an optional CRM identity link can be attached only when supplied through an authorized integration or first-party action.
- A live list of online visitors and current session state.
- Approximate geography, device/browser, referrer, and UTM campaign attributes. Geolocation is approximate and must not rely on durable raw IP storage.
- Page history, current page, active engagement time, and session lifecycle events.
- CTA interactions and application-defined custom events.
- A visitor-detail timeline that combines tracking, engagement, chat, and call events in chronological order.
- Basic, explainable intent scoring based on configured session signals such as recency, engagement, page/category, and CTA events.

### Agent engagement

- Agent-to-visitor text chat with real-time delivery and durable conversation history.
- Agent-initiated browser voice and video call invitations.
- Visitor-controlled call states: pending/ringing, accepted, declined, cancelled, missed, and ended.
- A visitor must explicitly accept an invitation before a media connection is created and must grant browser microphone/camera permissions through the browser.
- Durable call history and metadata, excluding media recordings.

### Platform foundations

- Tenant/site authorization boundaries for agents and public tracker requests.
- Rate limiting and allowed-origin validation on public tracking endpoints.
- Operational error handling that returns safe public errors without implementation details or secrets.

## Explicitly out of scope for V1

- Automatic personal identity discovery or enrichment from an IP address.
- Audio or video recording, storage, transcription, or playback.
- Co-browsing or remote browser control.
- Session replay.
- Heatmaps.
- PSTN calling, phone-number routing, or SIP integration.
- An AI agent that autonomously chats or calls visitors.
- Native iOS or Android applications.

## Product boundaries and assumptions

- V1 supports modern browsers capable of Server-Sent Events and WebRTC; progressive degradation for unsupported browsers is limited to non-media tracking and chat where possible.
- Calls are browser-to-browser sessions coordinated by the application and carried by LiveKit; the Next.js application never carries media bytes.
- CRM linking is optional and represents an external identity supplied by an authorized system. A CRM connector and broad CRM synchronization are not required for V1.
- Consent notices, cookie choices, data-retention periods, and regional compliance configuration remain product/legal decisions to be finalized before production launch.
